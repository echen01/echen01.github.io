MRI Comparison with Speech2rtMRI

We evaluate our MRI reconstruct technique on examples from the USC 75-Speaker Speech MRI dataset. Background noise from the MRI machine is removed with Adobe Express’s speech enhancement tool. By “analysis by synthesis”, our technique can reconstruct both a MRI video of the speaker, and the speech synthesized from our acoustic simulator. On the other hand, the SOTA MRI reconstruction method Speech2rtMRI [Nguyen et al., 2025] cannot synthesize audio. Speech2rtMRI suffers from significant degradation because it is a video diffusion model with no acoustic grounding for the human vocal tract. Video diffusion models are known to struggle when generating long sequences. Meanwhile, our method can faithfully reconstruct the MRIs. One major difficulty when solving this problem is that the mapping between vocal tract geometry to speech is not one-to-one. Multiple vocal tract shapes can product the same sound, causing ambiguity in the generated MRIs.

Audio is sampled at 24000 Hz.
